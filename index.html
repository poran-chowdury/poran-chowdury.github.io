04 · System design

How I design backend systems

The reference shape I build toward: thin edges, independent services, events for side effects, and money that can only move once.

  • Edges stay thin: authentication, rate limits and tenant resolution happen once, at the gateway.
  • Money moves once: idempotency keys, owner-checked locks and terminal-state guards.
  • Side effects go through events: a transactional outbox, retries and dead-letter queues.
  • Trust is verified: reconciliation workers compare against the source of truth.
